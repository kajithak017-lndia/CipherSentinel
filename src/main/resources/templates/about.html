🛡️ About CipherSentinel

CipherSentinel is an AI-Powered Document Integrity Detection System developed to identify document forgery, integrity violations, suspicious modifications, and missing critical information in Land Records, Legal Documents, and Financial Statements.

The platform combines OCR-based text extraction, SHA-256 cryptographic verification, template validation, similarity analysis, anomaly detection, trust scoring, audit monitoring, and automated email alerts to ensure document authenticity and integrity.

By continuously validating uploaded documents against trusted document structures and integrity fingerprints, CipherSentinel helps users detect unauthorized modifications and potential tampering attempts before they can cause damage.

✨ Key Features

  • 📄 Land Record Verification
  • ⚖️ Legal Document Validation
  • 💰 Financial Statement Analysis
  • 🔍 OCR-Based Text Extraction
